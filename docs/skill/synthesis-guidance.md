# Synthesis Guidance

Use this before Synthesis.

`synthesis.md` is the curator's framing of the domain. It is copied into the top of `MIXTAPE.md`, so it should teach the consuming AI how to read the source list.

## What To Write

A useful synthesis usually includes:

- the frame or model for the domain
- the main principles or distinctions
- contested questions and where the mixtape leans
- what the corpus is good for
- what the corpus does not cover

## What To Avoid

- A source-by-source recap.
- A generic topic overview.
- Motivational prose.
- Claims that are broader than the source list can support.
- Placeholder language like "the curator should add..."

## Voice

Use direct prose. First person is fine when it clarifies the curator's stance. Hedging is fine when the evidence is genuinely mixed, but do not dilute every claim into mush.

## Length

Aim for 800-2000 words for a serious mixtape. Shorter is acceptable for a small personal mixtape if it still gives the AI a real frame.

Write plain Markdown with a top-level heading.
