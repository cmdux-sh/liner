# Candidate long-list

Phase 2 candidate pool for the capability brief. URLs were checked for existence by direct web fetch or an alternate search result on 2026-10-02. This is still an unfiltered long-list: no substantive source content has been fetched into the project for evaluation yet.

The three existing tape URLs are included as curator-provided seeds. Candidate reasons name the Required source role each source may satisfy; final keep/trim/drop decisions belong to Phase 4.

## Coverage snapshot

- **Calibrated-reliance foundations:** 8 candidates; includes primary and foundational human-factors research.
- **Uncertainty representation and confidence limits:** 9 candidates; includes experiments, technical uncertainty work, and the three curator seeds.
- **Evidence and verification interaction methods:** 8 candidates; includes citation, attribution, provenance, and inspectability research.
- **Worked interface cases across core output types:** 8 candidates; spans summaries/answers, recommendations, extraction, and general AI interaction patterns across Google, Microsoft, Apple, IBM, and Salesforce.
- **Deferral, safeguards, and human-control authority:** 8 candidates; includes NIST, W3C, ICO, and product-level fallback guidance.
- **Critique and validation evidence:** 9 candidates; includes over-reliance, automation-bias, negative findings, design-process research, a frontline clinical perspective, and a video overview.

## Section 1: The reliance decision

  - **Candidate reason:** Foundational review that may define appropriate reliance, trust dynamics, and display effects for imperfect automation.

- **Humans and Automation: Use, Misuse, Disuse, Abuse** — https://web.mit.edu/16.459/www/parasuraman.pdf
  - **Source role:** Calibrated-reliance foundations; critique and validation evidence
  - **Candidate reason:** Primary human-factors account that may distinguish over-reliance, under-use, workload, risk, and monitoring failures.

- **Trust in Automation: Integrating Empirical Evidence on Factors That Influence Trust** — https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1163926
  - **Source role:** Calibrated-reliance foundations
  - **Candidate reason:** Systematic review that may connect observed human-subjects evidence to a layered model of trust and reliance.

- **Algorithm Appreciation: People Prefer Algorithmic to Human Judgment** — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0749597818303388
  - **Source role:** Calibrated-reliance foundations; critique and validation evidence
  - **Candidate reason:** Multi-study evidence that may prevent a simplistic assumption that users always resist algorithmic advice.

- **Algorithm Aversion: People Erroneously Avoid Algorithms After Seeing Them Err** — https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/20-Dietvorst-Simmons-Massey-2015.pdf
  - **Source role:** Calibrated-reliance foundations; critique and validation evidence
  - **Candidate reason:** Primary experiments that may explain how visible mistakes change reliance even when an algorithm outperforms a person.

- **Beyond Accuracy: The Role of Mental Models in Human-AI Team Performance** — https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/HCOMP/article/view/5285
  - **Source role:** Calibrated-reliance foundations; worked interface cases
  - **Candidate reason:** Primary human-AI team study that may tie knowledge of an AI error boundary to accept/override behavior and joint performance.

- **To Trust or to Think: Cognitive Forcing Functions Can Reduce Overreliance on AI in AI-Assisted Decision-Making** — https://www.iis.seas.harvard.edu/papers/2021/bucinca2021trust.shtml
  - **Source role:** Calibrated-reliance foundations; critique and validation evidence
  - **Candidate reason:** Primary experiment that may show when explanations fail to reduce over-reliance and how deliberate friction changes behavior.

- **Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction** — https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf
  - **Source role:** Calibrated-reliance foundations; worked interface cases; critique and validation evidence
