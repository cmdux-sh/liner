# Synthesis

This is an example mixtape demonstrating Liner's project-folder format. Replace
this synthesis with the curator's distilled view of the domain before treating
the compiled MIXTAPE.md as a finished artifact.

A real mobile-design synthesis would cover:

- The HIG/Material divergence on touch targets, gestures, and navigation, and
  where each one is conservative or aggressive relative to platform conventions.
- Why bottom-tab navigation became the dominant pattern on mobile and where it
  breaks down (deep hierarchies, multi-tasking, large-screen iPad layouts).
- The relationship between system gestures and app-defined gestures — the
  hierarchy that makes back-gesture and pull-to-refresh feel right or wrong.
- When to deviate from platform conventions for product distinctiveness, and
  when deviation is just friction.

Treat this synthesis as the curator's framing. The AI consuming this mixtape
should use it to anchor responses, and load specific source files when the
conversation calls for platform-specific detail.
