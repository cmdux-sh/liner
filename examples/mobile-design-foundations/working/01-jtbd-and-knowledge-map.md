# JTBD and knowledge map

## Job-to-be-done

When I'm designing the core UI of a consumer iOS app, I want to follow Apple's interaction conventions while choosing where to deviate visually, so I can ship something that feels native without looking generic.

## Knowledge map

- Foundations
  - Touch and gesture models on iOS vs. Material
  - System-level vs. app-level affordances
  - Accessibility implications of common patterns
- Patterns
  - Bottom-tab vs. drawer vs. hub-and-spoke navigation
  - Pull-to-refresh, swipe actions, long-press menus
  - Modal vs. push transitions
- Craft
  - Typography in small viewports
  - Color and contrast under sunlight conditions
  - Motion choreography (timing, easing, hierarchy)
